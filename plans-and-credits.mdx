## Choose a plan

AI Puffer has a free version and three paid plans. **Pro**, **Pro Plus**, and **Pro Max** unlock the same Pro plugin features. All three subscription plans include monthly AI Puffer Cloud credits.

| Plan | Plugin features | Included AI credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Core tools | No paid-plan allowance. Eligible verified Cloud accounts receive 25 free credits monthly. |
| Pro | All Pro features | **1,000 AI credits monthly** with a paid monthly or annual subscription. |
| Pro Plus | All Pro features | **3,000 AI credits monthly**. |
| Pro Max | All Pro features | **10,000 AI credits monthly**. |

Cloud is optional on every plan. Using a direct provider with your own API key uses that provider's billing instead of your AI Puffer credits. Realtime voice uses a separate direct OpenAI connection.

## Monthly credits

- **Renews monthly:** Pro, Plus and Max credits renew each monthly period, including when you pay annually. An annual Plus subscription does not issue 36,000 credits upfront.
- **No rollover:** Unused included credits expire at the end of their monthly period.
- **Shared across sites:** A 2-site Pro subscription includes 1,000 monthly credits total. A 2-site Plus license includes 3,000 monthly credits total, not 3,000 per site. A 2-site Max license includes 10,000 total. Connect both sites to the same Cloud account to share the balance.
- **Connection required:** Activate the paid license, connect Cloud using the same Freemius account, and verify the email if requested. A plugin purchase does not automatically connect Cloud.
- **Separate free allowance:** Eligible verified accounts can also receive the 25-credit free monthly allowance. It is separate from included plan credits and follows its own renewal date.

In WordPress, open **AI Puffer > Usage > Overview** to see **Free**, **Included**, **Purchased**, and available credits. Use **Refresh** after a purchase or plan change. The displayed renewal dates are the dates to follow for your account.

### Credits are not a message count

One credit is not necessarily one reply, article, or image. Text requests use token pricing with a model-specific minimum. Longer output, conversation history, instructions, and knowledge context can increase the charge. Image, audio, and embedding models have their own rates.

An article with SEO fields and images can make several requests. Each Cloud request uses credits. See [request pricing and reservations](/cloud#what-a-request-costs).

## Top-ups

Optional one-time top-ups remain available on **Free, Pro, Pro Plus, and Pro Max**.

1. Open **AI Puffer > Usage > Overview**.
2. Click **Buy credits**.
3. Choose a pack and check that the checkout email matches the Cloud account you want to fund.
4. Complete checkout, return to WordPress, and refresh your balance.

Purchased credits **do not expire** and are shared across sites connected to the same Cloud account. A top-up does not upgrade your plugin license or change your monthly plan allowance. Free credits are used first, then included credits, then purchased credits.

See [top-up invoices](/user-portal#cloud-top-up-invoices).

## Existing lifetime licenses

Lifetime licenses are no longer offered. Existing lifetime Pro licenses retain their software entitlements. They include **no monthly paid-plan AI credits**. You can still connect Cloud, receive the eligible free allowance, and buy top-ups, or use your own provider keys.

Plus and Max require monthly or annual subscriptions. A lifetime Pro license does not become a recurring credit subscription automatically.

## Change or cancel a plan

Use **AI Puffer > Pricing** in WordPress or the [Freemius customer portal](https://customers.freemius.com/) to review available upgrades and billing options. Check Freemius's checkout total and effective date before confirming a change.

- **Upgrade:** Cloud reconciles the paid entitlement. It increases the current period's allowance when applicable; it does not repeatedly issue a fresh full allowance on each plan change.
- **Change site count:** The software license covers the selected number of sites. Changing from 1 to 2 sites does not multiply the plan's monthly credits.
- **Cancel auto-renewal:** Stops future subscription charges. Monthly credit renewals can continue during already paid coverage, including the remaining months of an annual subscription. They stop when that coverage ends.
- **Downgrade:** Future renewals follow the active paid subscription: Pro includes 1,000, Plus 3,000, and Max 10,000 monthly AI credits. Free has no paid-plan allowance. Purchased top-ups remain separate.
- **Refund:** Refunded payments can reverse associated credits. A refund does not create another monthly allowance.

Complimentary licenses and unpaid trials do not fund included plan credits. If a paid allowance is missing, confirm the license, connected account email, verification, and payment status, then refresh Usage.

## Website actions

[Manage my website](/manage-my-website) connects an MCP assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude to WordPress.

| Plan | Monthly website actions |
| --- | ---: |
| Free | 2,000 |
| Pro subscription | 10,000 |
| Pro Plus subscription | 25,000 |
| Pro Max subscription | 50,000 |

These are total allowances, including the Free base. The highest eligible paid subscription applies; multiple licences do not stack. Monthly and annual subscriptions both receive monthly actions. Existing lifetime licences retain their software features and receive the ordinary Free website-action allowance, not a paid subscription allowance. There is no welcome bonus.

Upgrades increase the current allowance without resetting usage. Paid downgrades take effect at the next monthly anniversary; cancellation retains paid access through the paid coverage date. Refunds, revocations and account closure can remove access sooner.

Website actions and AI credits are separate balances. A website action is a tool operation, not a whole article: reading a post, creating a draft and requesting publication are separate operations. Your assistant's own subscription or model charges are separate.

The monthly allowance is shared across all connected sites and assistants on the same account. Adding a site or reconnecting does not reset it. The period starts on first MCP enrollment and renews monthly. Unused monthly actions do not roll over. Open **AI Puffer > Usage** to see used, remaining, monthly allowance and renewal date. An operation still being reconciled can temporarily hold part of the allowance.

Website management is optional under **Settings > Connections** in MCP-enabled plugin releases. If the card is absent, update to an MCP-enabled release when available; the Cloud backend alone does not add the card to an older plugin.
